## 3. The 5-Stage Vertically Integrated Lifecycle Model

Infeworks owns the complete engineering value chain in-house:

1. **Process & Hydraulic Engineering:**
   - Advanced hydraulic modeling, pipe transient surge simulations, mass balance calculations, membrane projection, and statutory authority engineering submissions.
2. **In-House Workshop Prefabrication & Skid Assembly:**
   - Dedicated steel fabrication facility for high-pressure carbon and stainless steel pipe spools, custom pump skids, manifold prefabrication, and dynamic pressure testing.
3. **Heavy Electromechanical Installations:**
   - Large split-case/multistage pump sets, ATEX-rated explosion-proof systems, Motor Control Centers (MCC), Variable Frequency Drives (VFD), and surge suppression vessels.
4. **SCADA, Telemetry & Industrial Automation:**
   - Industrial PLC programming (Siemens, Schneider, ABB), remote GSM/Fiber telemetry data loggers, centralized command center dashboards, and automated safety interlocks.
5. **Commissioning & Long-Term O&M:**
   - Rigorous 72-hour continuous performance test run, chemical/bacteriological water analysis matching Egyptian Potable Water Standards, operator training, and 24/7 preventative maintenance.

## 4. The 6 Specialized Engineering Sectors

### Sector 01: Water Treatment & Desalination (محطات تحلية وتنقية المياه)

- **Scope:** Sea Water Reverse Osmosis (SWRO), Brackish Water Reverse Osmosis (BWRO), containerized mobile RO skids, food-grade mineralization, and iron/manganese removal plants.
- **Key Highlight:** Food-grade RO plants for sovereign food manufacturing complexes adhering to strict hygiene and mineralization benchmarks.

### Sector 02: Wastewater & Effluent Treatment (محطات معالجة مياه الصرف الصحي والصناعي)

- **Scope:** Industrial Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), compact modular Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), Moving Bed Biofilm Reactors (MBBR), and biological/chemical neutralization.
- **Key Highlight:** Zero-liquid discharge (ZLD) support and environmental compliance for industrial cities.

### Sector 03: Pumping Stations & Deep Wells (محطات الرفع وآبار المياه الجوفية)

- **Scope:** High-capacity booster stations, irrigation intake lifting stations, dry/wet well sewage lift stations, solar-powered deep well pumps, and dynamic surge protection.
- **Key Highlight:** Turnkey delivery and ongoing multi-year operations of 22 pumping stations for national agricultural reclamation in Toshka.

### Sector 04: Infrastructure Networks & Utility Pipelines (شبكات البنية التحتية وخطوط النقل)

- **Scope:** High-pressure ductile iron and HDPE water transmission mains, gravity sewer networks, storm drainage collectors, and integrated utility corridors.
- **Key Highlight:** Laying strategic water transmission trunk lines in North Sinai from Sheikh Zuweid to New Salam City.

### Sector 05: Electromechanical & Industrial MEP (الأعمال الكهروميكانيكية والتبريد الصناعي)

- **Scope:** Industrial cooling loops, ammonia/freon cold storage utility networks, electrical grounding/cathodic protection, substations, and automated pump house MEP.
- **Key Highlight:** Turnkey utility networks and process cooling for major sovereign cold storage and agricultural logistics hubs.

### Sector 06: Sovereign, Civil & Environmental Infrastructure (المشروعات السيادية والأعمال المدنية)

- **Scope:** Sovereign landmark construction, integrated community utility networks, Bedouin housing utility zones, sanitary landfill environmental lining, and reinforced concrete reservoirs.
- **Key Highlight:** Turnkey construction of the Grand Mosque & Community Complex in the New Administrative Capital R3.

## 5. Major Delivered Flagship Case Studies

### 1. Toshka Mega Agricultural Reclamation — 22 Pumping Stations (توشكى — منظومة 22 محطة رفع)

- **Client / Authority:** National Service Projects Organization (NSPO) / Armed Forces Engineering Authority
- **Location:** Toshka, Aswan Governorate
- **Scope:** Turnkey engineering, mechanical procurement, electrical automation, and commissioning of 22 irrigation pumping stations across Toshka's desert footprint, followed by a multi-year continuous O&M mandate.
- **Delivered Outcome:** 100% operational readiness delivering uninterrupted irrigation supply for tens of thousands of feddans in Egypt's premier sovereign agricultural project.

### 2. Sadat City Food-Grade RO Desalination Plant (محطة تحلية السادات — مجمع الصناعات الغذائية)

- **Client / Authority:** NSPO Food Industries Complex (Silofoods / Food City)
- **Location:** Sadat City, Menoufia Governorate
- **Capacity:** 1,500 m³/day Food-Grade Potable Water
- **Scope:** Complete reverse osmosis train design, stainless steel 316L hygienic skid assembly, automated CIP (Clean-In-Place) systems, and statutory microbiological compliance testing.
- **Delivered Outcome:** Highly purified, food-grade process water meeting strict statutory food production parameters for biscuit and macaroni production lines.

### 3. Food City Industrial Effluent Treatment Facility (محطة معالجة الصرف الصناعي بالمدينة الغذائية)

- **Client / Authority:** National Service Projects Organization
- **Location:** Sadat City, Menoufia
- **Capacity:** 50 m³/day Concentrated Industrial Effluent Treatment
- **Scope:** Chemical coagulation, biological degradation, automated sludge handling, and SCADA telemetry.
- **Delivered Outcome:** Complete compliance with environmental discharge standards, safeguarding municipal drainage from industrial process waste.

### 4. Arish City Comprehensive Water Supply Networks (شبكات مياه الشرب الشاملة بالعريش)

- **Client / Authority:** North Sinai Development Authority / Holding Company for Water (HCWW)
- **Location:** Arish, North Sinai Governorate
- **Scope:** Laying primary distribution networks, booster pump installations, valves chambers, and pressure monitoring stations.
- **Delivered Outcome:** Modernized potable water infrastructure providing continuous, reliable supply to residential and civic districts in North Sinai.

### 5. Ameriya Cold-Storage Logistics Complex (مجمع ثلاجات العامرية اللوجستي)

- **Client / Authority:** Alexandria Agricultural & Cold Storage Mandate
- **Location:** Ameriya, Alexandria Governorate
- **Scope:** Industrial process cooling piping, condenser water circulation loops, industrial drainage, and dynamic balancing manifolds.
- **Delivered Outcome:** Stable, high-efficiency thermal exchange and utility reliability for national food storage reserves.

### 6. Qabs Min Nour Grand Mosque & Civic Complex (مسجد قبس من نور الجامع — العاصمة الإدارية)

- **Client / Authority:** Armed Forces Engineering Authority
- **Location:** R3 District, New Administrative Capital (العاصمة الإدارية الجديدة)
- **Scope:** Full turnkey civil contracting, architectural dome engineering, electro-mechanical MEP, integrated water storage, and surrounding civic utility networks.
- **Delivered Outcome:** An iconic sovereign landmark delivered under strict Engineering Authority standards.

### 7. New Salam City High-Pressure Water Pipeline (خط نقل مياه مدينة السلام — الشيخ زويد)

- **Client / Authority:** North Sinai Sovereign Development
- **Location:** Sheikh Zuweid to New Salam City, Sinai
