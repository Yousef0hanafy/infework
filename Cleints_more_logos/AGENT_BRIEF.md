## Rules I followed while sourcing (important for another agent to know)

1. **No hallucinated marks.** Every asset was fetched from either the entity's official website (C04, C15) or its official social page (C16, C17). Nothing was drawn from scratch, generated by an image model, or reconstructed by memory.
2. **Standardization is content-preserving.** Assets were trimmed of transparent/white padding, resized to fit the 1400 × 800 safe area (aspect-preserving), then centered on a 1600 × 1000 transparent canvas. No color changes, no logo edits, no stylization. The white-JPG variant is just the same PNG flattened onto `#ffffff`.
3. **Ambiguous registry tokens are flagged, not resolved.** C15/C16/C17 came from three single-word owner-column tokens (`الشرق`, `الزاهي`, `النوبي`). Each maps to exactly one live Egyptian entity that I could find under that exact wordmark — but that mapping is a *best candidate*, not a *confirmed match*. The manifest marks these as `plausible_candidate_from_official_page` for a reason: the client's source document should be the tiebreaker.
4. **The composite records (C18, C19) are not in this bundle.** They were addressed separately in the main project (as `18_joint_...` and `19_joint_...` — 3-up and 2-up renders of the C15/C16/C17 member logos). If you need those, ask.
