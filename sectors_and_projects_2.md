## 1. Portfolio Asset Reality: Authentic vs. Recycled Image Breakdown

To maintain a polished, non-empty frontend presentation across all 43 cataloged projects, images were historically divided into two distinct groups:

- **16 Authentic Projects (37%):** Have genuine, verified field photographs taken directly from Infeworks job sites.
- **27 Sector-Pooled Projects (63%):** Had **no original photos** in the raw archive. To avoid blank/empty cards, they were dynamically assigned a pool of images borrowed from authentic projects within the same engineering sector.

## 5. Action Plan for Replacing Recycled Photos

When you have real photos ready for any of the 27 projects above:

1. **Drop New Photos:** Create/place the webp images directly inside `public/images/projects/<slug>/` named `cover.webp`, `gallery-1.webp`, `gallery-2.webp`, etc.
2. **Flag as Authentic:** Add the project slug to the `AUTHENTIC_PROJECT_SLUGS` list in [`src/lib/project-meta.ts`](src/lib/project-meta.ts).
3. **No Code Rebuilding Needed:** The frontend and portfolio gallery will instantly recognize the new authentic images.
