Method statement for HDPE (350-500) pipes
1. Introduction
This method statement outlines the procedure for installing a water pipeline using High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) pipes with a diameter of 350 and 500 mm, in compliance with the Egyptian Code
2. Tools and Equipment Required
HDPE pipes (500 mm diameter).
Welding tools (butt fusion or electrofusion machines).
Excavation equipment (excavators or backhoes).
Lifting and handling equipment.
Measuring tapes and leveling tools.
Power generators.
Soil compactors.
Backfilling materials (fine sand, suitable soil).
Personal protective equipment (PPE).
3. Procedure
3.1 Planning and Preparation
Review design drawings and ensure compliance with project requirements.
Mark the pipeline route and ensure it is free of obstructions.
Conduct a field survey to establish fixed points (benchmarks) and measure the natural ground level (NGL).
3.2 Excavation
Mark and excavate to the required depth as per the drawings, considering the minimum cover depth specified by the Egyptian Code.
Perform excavation using appropriate machinery, maintaining side slopes to prevent trench collapse.
Dispose of surplus soil in designated areas.
3.3 Preparation of Trench Bottom
Clean the trench bottom to remove debris, rocks, or sharp objects.
Lay a bedding layer of fine sand, approximately 15-20 cm thick.
Level and compact the bedding layer.
3.4 Pipe Installation
Transport the HDPE pipes to the site using appropriate lifting equipment to avoid damage.
Assemble pipes using butt fusion or electrofusion welding techniques, following the manufacturer’s guidelines.
Inspect the welded joints to ensure they are strong and defect-free.
3.5 Pipe Testing
Perform a hydrostatic pressure test to ensure the system is leak-free:
Test pressure should be 1.5 times the operating pressure.
Maintain the pressure for a minimum of two hours.
Repair any detected leaks and retest until the system is confirmed leak-free.
3.6 Backfilling
Place a layer of fine sand around the pipe, covering it by 20-30 cm.
Backfill in layers not exceeding 30 cm using suitable materials, compacting each layer.
Ensure no sharp objects or stones are present in the backfill material.
3.7 Site Restoration
Level the surface area to match the surrounding ground.
Remove any waste materials and return unused equipment to storage.
Confirm the site has been restored to its original condition.
4. Safety Considerations
Use appropriate PPE (helmets, gloves, safety glasses).
Install warning signs around excavation zones.
Secure pipes during installation to prevent movement.
5. Documentation and Records
Submit hydrostatic test reports.
Provide a final report including all executed steps and verification against the Egyptian Code.
Method statement for Corrugated HDPE Pipes Lines for Sewage (200-900) mm
1. Introduction
This method statement outlines the step-by-step procedure for installing corrugated High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) pipes for sewage systems.
2. Tools and Equipment
Corrugated HDPE pipes (as per design specifications).
Flexible couplings or rubber gaskets.
Excavators and backhoes.
Lifting and handling equipment.
Leveling tools (spirit level, laser level).
Compaction equipment.
Backfilling materials (fine sand, granular soil).
Pressure testing equipment.
Personal protective equipment (PPE).
3. Procedure
3.1 Planning and Preparations
Site Inspection:
Ensure the site is clear of obstacles.
Verify pipe sizes, grades, and alignments from the approved drawings.
Material Check:
Inspect all pipes, fittings, and gaskets for damages before use.
Ensure all materials meet the required specifications.
Survey:
Mark the alignment and level for the pipeline using a total station or other surveying tools.
3.2 Excavation
Trench Dimensions:
Excavate the trench to the required depth, width, and slope as per the design.
Maintain side slopes to prevent collapses.
Base Preparation:
Remove debris, rocks, and sharp objects from the trench bottom.
Lay a bedding layer of fine sand or granular material (minimum 10-15 cm thick).
Compact the bedding layer uniformly.
3.3 Pipe Installation
Laying Pipes:
Transport pipes carefully using lifting equipment to avoid damage.
Place pipes on the prepared bedding layer with correct alignment and slope (minimum 1% slope for gravity lines).
Joint Assembly:
Clean the pipe ends and gaskets to ensure proper sealing.
Use flexible couplings or rubber gaskets to join pipes, following the manufacturer’s guidelines.
Push pipes together using manual force or equipment, ensuring alignment.
Inspection:
Check each joint for proper installation and sealing.
Ensure the pipeline maintains the required gradient.
3.4 Testing
Leakage Test:
Conduct a low-pressure air test or water test to check for leaks.
Follow the specifications for the test pressure and duration.
Repair any detected leaks and retest if necessary.
3.5 Backfilling
Initial Backfill:
Cover the pipe with granular soil or fine sand to a height of 20-30 cm above the pipe.
Compact the material gently to avoid damaging the pipe.
Final Backfill:
Use selected backfill material free of rocks or sharp objects.
Backfill in layers (maximum 30 cm) and compact each layer to the required density.
3.6 Site Restoration
Restore the surface to its original condition.
Dispose of excess materials and clean up the site.
4. Safety Considerations
All workers must wear appropriate PPE (helmets, gloves, safety boots, high-visibility vests).
Install warning signs and barriers around the work area.
Ensure trench safety by shoring or sloping as required.
Avoid working in trenches during adverse weather conditions.
Method Statement for Cast-in-Situ Concrete Manhole on Sewage Pipelines (Diameter < 500 mm, Manhole Internal Diameter = 1 meter)
1. Introduction
This method statement describes the step-by-step procedure for constructing a cast-in-situ concrete manhole on sewage pipelines with a diameter less than 500 mm, ensuring the manhole internal
2. Materials and Equipment Required
Materials
Portland cement (Type I or as per design requirements).
Sand and coarse aggregate (clean and graded).
Formwork materials (plywood, steel panels, or timber).
PVC or HDPE pipe connectors for pipeline interfaces.
Waterproofing materials (bituminous coating or equivalent).
Cement mortar (1:3 ratio) for finishing and sealing.
Manhole cover and frame (cast iron or ductile iron).
Equipment
Excavator and loader
Concrete mixer or ready-mix concrete delivery.
Vibrator for concrete compaction.
Measuring tools (spirit level, laser level).
Compaction equipment.
Cutting tools for pipeline adjustments.
3. Procedure
3.1 Site Preparation
Survey and Marking:
Mark the exact location of the manhole based on design drawings.
Confirm alignment and levels of incoming and outgoing pipelines.
Material Inspection:
Verify quality and quantity of all materials.
Ensure reinforcement steel meets the specifications.
3.2 Excavation
Dimensions:
Excavate to the required depth and width to accommodate the manhole base, walls, and backfill.
Allow additional space for formwork installation.
Trench Safety:
Provide sloping or shoring to prevent soil collapse.
Trench Bottom Preparation:
Level and compact the bottom of the excavation.
Lay a 100-150 mm thick layer of compacted granular material as a bedding.
3.3 Base Construction
Reinforcement Placement:
Place reinforcement steel for the base as per the design.
Ensure proper cover (minimum 50 mm) using spacers.
Concrete Casting:
Pour concrete for the base (minimum thickness: 150-200 mm).
Compact the concrete using a vibrator to eliminate air voids.
Level the surface and leave sufficient time for curing (at least 24 hours).
3.4 Wall Construction
Formwork Installation:
Erect circular or rectangular formwork for the manhole walls with an internal diameter of 1 meter.
Secure the formwork to avoid movement during casting.
Concrete Casting:
Pour concrete in layers (300-500 mm per lift) to avoid segregation.
Compact each layer with a vibrator for uniformity and strength.
Allow the concrete to set and cure for at least 24 hours before removing the formwork.
3.5 Pipeline Connections
Pipe Openings:
Create openings in the manhole wall during casting or drill post-casting.
Pipe Interface:
Install PVC or HDPE connectors to join the pipelines to the manhole.
Seal the interface with cement mortar or a waterproof sealant.
3.6 Benching and Channel Formation
Channel Construction:
Shape the internal flow channel at the base of the manhole using cement mortar.
Ensure a smooth finish and proper slope toward the outlet pipe.
Benching:
Construct benching at an angle (minimum 1:12) for smooth flow and maintenance access.
3.7 Cover and Frame Installation
Placement:
Position the cast iron or ductile iron frame on top of the manhole.
Level the frame with the surrounding ground level.
Sealing:
Seal the joint between the frame and concrete with mortar.
3.8 Backfilling
Material and Procedure:
Use suitable backfill material free of sharp objects or debris.
Backfill in layers (not exceeding 30 cm) and compact each layer to the required density.
Surface Restoration:
Restore the surface to its original condition or as per project requirements.
4. Safety Considerations
Ensure all workers wear PPE (helmets, gloves, boots, vests).
Provide proper shoring or sloping to prevent trench collapses.
Use barricades and warning signs around the excavation.
Ensure safe handling of reinforcement steel and concrete materials.
5. Quality Control
Inspect reinforcement placement and formwork alignment before casting.
Verify concrete quality through slump and compressive strength tests.
Ensure proper curing of concrete for at least 7 days.
6. Documentation
Record all levels, dimensions, and alignments of the manhole.
Submit inspection and testing reports for reinforcement and concrete.
Provide photographic records of construction stages.
Method Statement for Construction of Manhole on Sewage Pipelines (Diameter > 500 mm)
1. Introduction
This method statement outlines the procedure for constructing a manhole on sewage pipelines with diameters exceeding 500 mm
2. Materials and Equipment Required
Materials
Portland cement (Type I or Type II, as per design requirements).
Sand and coarse aggregate (clean and graded).
Reinforcement steel (as per design specifications).
Formwork materials (plywood, steel panels, or timber).
PVC or HDPE pipe connectors (for pipeline interfaces).
Waterproofing materials (bituminous coating or equivalent).
Cement mortar (1:3 mix ratio) for finishing and sealing.
Manhole cover and frame (heavy-duty cast iron or ductile iron).
Equipment
Excavator and backhoe.
Concrete mixer or ready-mix concrete delivery.
Vibrator for concrete compaction.
Lifting equipment for heavy pipes and manhole components.
Measuring tools (spirit level, laser level).
Compaction equipment.
Cutting tools for pipeline adjustments.
3. Procedure
3.1 Site Preparation
Survey and Marking:
Mark the location of the manhole as per design drawings.
Confirm the alignment and levels of the incoming and outgoing pipelines.
Material Inspection:
Inspect all materials, including reinforcement, formwork, and pipes, to ensure they meet the project specifications.
3.2 Excavation
Dimensions:
Excavate the area to the required depth and width, allowing space for the manhole base, walls, and formwork installation.
Provide additional clearance for safe working and backfilling.
Trench Safety:
Install shoring or sloping to prevent trench collapse, especially for deep excavations.
Base Preparation:
Level and compact the bottom of the excavation.
Lay a bedding layer of granular material or lean concrete (minimum thickness: 150 mm) to support the base.
3.3 Manhole Base Construction
Reinforcement Placement:
Place reinforcement steel as per the design, ensuring proper cover using spacers (minimum 50 mm).
Concrete Casting:
Cast a reinforced concrete base with a thickness of at least 200 mm or as specified in the design.
Compact the concrete using a vibrator to eliminate air voids.
Allow the concrete to cure for at least 24 hours before proceeding.
3.4 Manhole Wall Construction
Formwork Installation:
Install circular or rectangular formwork with the specified internal diameter (typically 1.5 meters or larger).
Ensure the formwork is secure and properly aligned.
Reinforcement Steel:
Place reinforcement steel for the walls as per the structural design.
Provide openings for the incoming and outgoing pipelines.
Concrete Casting:
Pour concrete in layers (300-500 mm per lift) to ensure proper compaction.
Use a vibrator to achieve a dense and uniform structure.
Allow the walls to cure for at least 24-48 hours before removing the formwork.
3.5 Pipeline Connections
Pipe Openings:
Create openings in the manhole walls during casting or drill post-casting.
Interface Connection:
Install pipe connectors (e.g., flexible seals or gaskets) to join the pipelines to the manhole.
Seal around the openings with cement mortar or a waterproofing material to prevent infiltration or exfiltration.
3.6 Benching and Flow Channel Formation
Channel Construction:
Shape the internal flow channel in the base to guide sewage flow.
Ensure the channel has a smooth finish and matches the pipeline gradient and diameter.
Benching:
Construct the benching with cement mortar, sloped at a minimum of 1:12 to prevent debris accumulation and facilitate maintenance access.
3.7 Cover and Frame Installation
Placement:
Install a heavy-duty cast iron or ductile iron frame and cover on top of the manhole.
Adjust the frame to match the surrounding ground level.
Sealing:
Seal the frame-to-concrete joint with mortar or epoxy grout.
3.8 Backfilling
Material and Layers:
Backfill around the manhole with selected material free of sharp objects and debris.
Compact in layers not exceeding 300 mm to achieve the required density.
Surface Restoration:
Restore the surface to its original condition or as per project requirements.
4. Safety Considerations
Ensure all workers use appropriate PPE (helmets, gloves, safety boots, high-visibility vests).
Provide proper ventilation and gas testing before working inside the manhole.
Use barricades and warning signs around the work area.
Ensure safe handling and lifting of heavy materials and equipment.
5. Quality Control
Inspect reinforcement and formwork alignment before casting.
Verify concrete quality through slump and compressive strength tests.
Check the alignment and sealing of pipeline connections.
Confirm the manhole dimensions and gradient as per design.
