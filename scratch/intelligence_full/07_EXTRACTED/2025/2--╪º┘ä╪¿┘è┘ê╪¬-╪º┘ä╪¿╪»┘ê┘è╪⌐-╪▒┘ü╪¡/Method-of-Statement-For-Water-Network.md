METHOD STATEMENT FOR WATER NETWORK
METHOD STATEMENT FOR SUPPLY, INSTALLATION, TESTING & COMMISSIONING OF WATER NETWORK INSTALLATION
1. Purpose and Scope
This Method Statement describes the procedures, resources, materials, quality controls, safety requirements, and responsibilities necessary for the supply, installation, testing, flushing, disinfection, and commissioning of HDPE water pipelines and associated fittings including elbows, tees, reducers, valves, house connection valves, and fire hydrants.
The scope covers:
Excavation, backfilling, and compaction works.
Supply and installation of HDPE pipes (110 mm & 160 mm) with pressure rating PN10.
Supply and installation of all special fittings of matching HDPE material.
Installation of ductile iron valves PN10 and plastic/bronze valves for house connections.
Installation of fire hydrants (Dry Barrel Type) with all accessories.
Pressure testing, flushing, reinstatement, and final handover.
Compliance with the Egyptian Code of Water Works (May 2010), Ministerial Decree 277/2000, approved drawings, and project specifications.
2. Preliminary Preparations and Material Inspection
2.1 General Preparations
Review approved IFC drawings, specifications, and project quality plan.
Verify site access, traffic management plans, and work permits.
Identify existing underground utilities using approved detection methods.
Mobilize required manpower, machinery (excavator, compactor, generator, fusion machine), and tools.
2.2 Material Inspection
All materials shall be inspected upon delivery:
HDPE Pipes & Fittings
Verify manufacturer’s certificates and compliance with Egyptian standards.
Check pressure rating PN10, wall thickness (SDR), dimensions, and color coding.
Ensure fittings (elbows, tees, reducers, end caps, couplers, rubber rings) are from the same material and approved vendor.
Valves
DI gate valves PN10 (100 mm & 150 mm) – check operation, coating, bolts, and gaskets.
Plastic/Bronze valves for house connections – verify size 40×40 cm GRP/plastic cover.
Fire Hydrants
Dry Barrel fire hydrant, 6-inch inlet with 3 outlets (1 × 4" & 2 × 2.5"), approved by Civil Defense.
3. Execution and Installation Methodology
3.1 Excavation Works
Excavate trenches according to approved alignment and Egyptian Code widths.
Maximum trench depth as per drawings, with safe side slopes or shoring where required.
Dispose of unsuitable excavated soil to an approved dump site.
Protect adjacent utilities and structures.
3.2 Bedding & Pipe Laying
Provide a sand bedding layer minimum 10 cm.
Lower HDPE pipes using fabric slings to avoid damage.
Ensure proper alignment, gradient, and spacing.
Perform butt fusion or electrofusion joints using certified technicians.
Record fusion parameters for each joint (temperature, pressure, heating time).
3.3 Backfilling
First layer: fine sand up to 30 cm above pipe crown.
Continue backfilling in layers not exceeding 25 cm thickness.
Compact each layer to 95% MDD according to field & lab tests.
Avoid dropping heavy materials directly over the pipe.
3.4 Installation of Valves
Ductile Iron Valves (100 & 150 mm)
Provide protective casing pipe, surface box, and stem extension.
Fix bolts, gaskets, and accessories as per manufacturer requirements.
House Connection Valves
Install plastic/bronze valve with approved cover (40 × 40 cm).
Ensure valves are accessible for operation.
3.5 Fire Hydrant Installation
Excavate and prepare concrete base.
Install 6-inch dry barrel hydrant with 4-inch protection pipe and required fittings.
Install isolating valves, elbows, and tees as per drawings.
Backfill and compact in layers.
3.6 Cleaning and Site Restoration
Remove excess soil, debris, welding residues.
Restore road surfaces, paving, or landscaping to original condition.
4. Testing and Commissioning
4.1 Pressure Test
Fill pipeline slowly and leave for 24 hours for saturation.
Apply test pressure as per Egyptian Code (minimum 1.5 × operating pressure).
Monitor pressure drop and inspect for leaks.
Record test results in official test sheets.
4.4 Commissioning
Open valves gradually and place the system into operation.
Ensure hydrants and valves operate smoothly.
5. Completion and Handover
Submit as-built drawings, test certificates, material approvals, fusion logs, and inspection reports.
Conduct final walkthrough with consultant.
Rectify punch list items.
Obtain final acceptance for the full HDPE water network system.
6. Safety and Environmental Precautions
All workers must wear PPE (helmets, vests, gloves, safety shoes).
Provide trench shoring where required and maintain safe access.
Barricade the work area and install warning signs and lights.
Dispose of excess excavated soil in approved locations.
Follow emergency response plan for fire, leakage, or collapse.
