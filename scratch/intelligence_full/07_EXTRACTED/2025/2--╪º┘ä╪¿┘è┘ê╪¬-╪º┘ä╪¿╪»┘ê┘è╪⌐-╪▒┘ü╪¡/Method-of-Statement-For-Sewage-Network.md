METHOD STATEMENT FOR SEWAGE NETWORK
METHOD STATEMENT FOR SUPPLY, INSTALLATION, TESTING & COMMISSIONING OF UPVC SN8 GRAVITY SEWER NETWORK
Purpose and Scope
This Method Statement describes in detail the procedures and methodology for the supply, installation, testing, commissioning, and handover of UPVC SN8 gravity sewer pipelines for the project’s underground sewage network.
The scope includes:
Excavation in all types of soil except rock.
Disposal of unsuitable excavated materials to approved dumping areas.
Supply of UPVC SN8 pipes and fittings in accordance with:
E.S 1717/2008 for diameters up to Ø630 mm and depths up to 4.50 m.
DIN 8061 & DIN 8062 for diameters greater than Ø630 mm or depths > 4.50 m.
Trench preparation, bedding, and installation of pipes.
Surrounding, haunching, and backfilling with proper compaction.
Testing (Water leakage)
Final reinstatement and handover.
Preliminary Preparations and Material Inspection
Drawing Review
Review IFC drawings, longitudinal profiles, cross sections, pipe slopes, invert levels, and manhole details.
Verify dimensions, gradients, and alignment.
Identify design discrepancies and raise RFI where needed.
Material Inspection
Compliance
UPVC pipes are SN8 stiffness class, complying with:
E.S 1717/2008 (≤ Ø630 mm)
DIN 8061/8062 (> Ø630 mm)
Quality Check
Manufacturer’s certificates
Physical inspection for cracks, scratches, deformation, or ovality.
Verification of Socket conditions
Verification of Rubber sealing rings
Verification of Wall thickness
Verification of Pipe stiffness
Storage
Pipes shall be stored on a firm, flat surface in a shaded area away from direct sunlight and sharp objects. Stacking height shall not exceed the manufacturer’s recommendation. Rubber rings and fittings shall be stored in clean, dry containers to prevent deformation or contamination.
Execution and Installation Methodology
Setting Out
Survey team to mark pipe centerline, trench width, invert levels, and manhole locations.
Engineer approval required before excavation.
Excavation Works
General Excavation
Excavate according to project drawings and Egyptian Code trench width requirements.
Maintain safe side slopes or provide shoring if required.
Remove all unsuitable materials.
Disposal
Suitable excavated material to be stockpiled for reuse.
Unsuitable material to be transported to approved disposal areas with official receipts.
Bedding & Foundation Preparation
Bedding Layer
Supply and install clean sand bedding beneath pipes, thickness based on:
Soil investigation report
Egyptian Code
Consultant recommendations
Approved drawings
Selected Fill / Replacement Layer (If required)
If natural soil is unsuitable, provide selected fill as replacement.
Material shall be approved, well-graded, free of organic matter, and compactable.
Pipe Installation
Lowering Pipes
Lower pipes carefully using manpower (small diameters) or mobile crane/excavator (large diameters).
Avoid dragging or impact.
Alignment & Positioning
Install pipes from downstream to upstream.
Ensure required gradient
Correct invert levels
Straight alignment
No unwanted sagging or uplift
Jointing
Clean spigot and socket thoroughly.
Ensure rubber ring is correctly positioned.
Apply approved lubricant (if applicable).
Insert pipe smoothly until insertion mark aligns.
Check joint tightness and alignment.
Surrounding & Initial Backfilling (Pipe Zone)
Use clean sand around pipe sides and above crown up to 30 cm minimum.
Compact manually using light compaction tools to avoid pipe movement.
Ensure no stones or debris are in the pipe zone.
Final Backfilling (Above Pipe Zone)
Using Suitable Excavated Material
If excavated material is suitable, it will be used for backfilling above pipe zone.
Imported Fill (If Excavated Material is Unsuitable)
Supply approved filling material from outside the site.
Material must be free of clay, organic matter, or oversized particles.
Compaction Requirements
Backfill in layers ≤ 25 cm loose thickness.
Apply water spraying as necessary.
Compact to at least 95% Maximum Dry Density (MDD).
Road Levels
Continue backfilling and compaction until road formation level or natural ground level is achieved.
COMPLETION & HANDOVER
Clean site and remove temporary works.
Ensure all backfilling and compaction tests are approved.
Prepare and submit inspection requests (IR)
Test reports
As-built drawings
Material approval records
TESTING & COMMISSIONING
Water Leakage Test
Plug ends of pipe section.
Fill with water slowly to required level.
Stabilization period as per standard.
Check joints and pipe body for leakage.
SAFETY & ENVIRONMENTAL PRECAUTIONS
Safety Measures
Provide PPE: helmet, safety shoes, gloves, reflective vests.
Warning tape
Night lighting
Environmental Protection
Dispose of unsuitable soil at approved dumps.
Minimize dust through water spraying.
Reduce noise pollution and work within allowed hours.
Pipe Installation Method Statement (HDPE Double-Wall Corrugated Pipes)
Trench Preparation
Excavate the trench to the required depth, width, and slope as per approved drawings.
Remove any stones or debris from the trench bottom.
Prepare a clean sand bedding layer according to the specified thickness.
Bedding Installation
Level and compact the sand bedding to provide a smooth and stable surface.
Pipe Lowering
Lower the pipes into the trench using lifting belts or suitable equipment — no dragging or pushing is allowed.
Ensure the pipe is placed in the correct orientation (socket facing upstream).
Pipe Alignment
Adjust alignment and slope using laser level or surveying tools.
Ensure proper straightness and continuity of the gradient along the pipeline.
Jointing (Welding)
Clean the pipe ends before jointing.
Perform welding according to the manufacturer’s instructions and approved method.
Allow sufficient cooling time before moving or surrounding the joint.
All welding shall be supervised by the site engineer.
Pipe Surrounding (Side & Top Bedding)
Place clean sand around both sides of the pipe and compact manually.
Continue sand backfilling up to 30 cm above the pipe crown.
Compact layers to ensure uniform support.
Final Backfilling
Complete backfilling using approved soil or imported fill if required.
Place backfill in layers not exceeding 25 cm.
Apply water to achieve the required compaction.
Final Checks
Verify alignment, slope, and joint conditions after installation.
Confirm pipe stability before closing the trench completely.
Pipe Installation Method Statement (Ductile Iron Pipes (DI) – Ø800 mm with Flanged Joint)
Trench Preparation
Excavate the trench to the required depth, width, and slope as per approved drawings and Egyptian Code.
Ensure the trench bottom is free of stones, debris, or sharp objects.
Place a bedding layer (sand or crushed stone) as recommended by soil report to provide stable and uniform support.
Bedding Installation
Level and lightly compact the bedding to ensure uniform support along the pipe.
Ensure bedding thickness complies with soil report recommendations.
Pipe Lowering
Lower the DI pipes using lifting belts, slings, or crane.
Avoid dragging, pushing, or dropping pipes.
Place the pipe in correct orientation (spigot/flange facing upstream).
Pipe Alignment
Verify alignment and slope using total station, laser level, or spirit level.
Ensure proper straightness and continuity of gradient along the pipeline.
Adjust pipe position as necessary before joining.
Joining (Flanged Connections)
Connect pipes using flanged joints according to manufacturer specifications and Egyptian Code.
Flanged Joint Procedure
Prepare Flange Faces: Clean and inspect flange faces; remove rust, debris, and old gasket material.
Install Gasket: Place approved rubber gasket between flange faces.
Align Flanges: Carefully align pipe flanges to avoid bending or torsion.
Insert Bolts: Insert bolts with washers in all flange holes.
Tighten Bolts: Use a crisscross pattern to evenly compress the gasket.
Torque Check: Apply torque to each bolt as specified by the manufacturer.
Inspection: Check alignment and verify sealing before backfilling.
All flanged connections must be supervised by the site engineer.
Pipe Surrounding and Haunching
Place clean sand or crushed stone around both sides of the pipe (haunching).
Compact manually to avoid pipe displacement.
Continue surrounding backfill up to 30 cm above the pipe crown.
Final Backfilling
Complete backfilling using approved excavated soil or imported fill if necessary.
Place backfill in layers not exceeding 25 cm loose thickness.
Compact each layer to achieve minimum 95% Maximum Dry Density (MDD).
Apply water as needed to achieve proper compaction.
Continue backfill up to road surface or original ground level.
Final Checks
Verify invert levels, gradient, and alignment after pipe installation.
Inspect all flanged joints for proper torque and sealing.
Confirm pipe stability before trench closure.
Method Statement for Construction of Concrete Manholes (Ø1.0 m & Ø1.2 m) According to Egyptian Standard ES 1717/2008
Purpose and Scope
This Method Statement describes in detail the procedures for supply, construction, and handover of circular concrete manholes for sewer pipelines with diameters less than 500 mm and depths up to 1.5 m.
The scope includes:
Excavation and side support in all soil types except rock.
Supply, fabrication, and installation of metallic and wooden formwork for foundations, walls, and slabs.
Placement of reinforced concrete (foundation, walls, slabs) with fair-face finish and proper benching slope.
Supply, fabrication, and installation of steel reinforcement for slabs up to 1.5 m depth.
External waterproofing using cold-applied bitumen, 3 layers, ≥400 μm after drying.
Supply and installation of G.R.P. manhole covers.
Supply and installation of cast iron ladder steps (7.25 kg each).
Backfilling with clean sand in layers ≤25 cm, with proper compaction and testing.
Inspection and final approval by supervising engineer.
All works shall comply with approved drawings, project specifications, and instructions from the supervising engineer.
Preliminary Preparations and Material Inspection
Drawing and Specification Review
Review approved construction drawings for manhole location, size, depth, and connection details with incoming and outgoing pipes. Confirm invert levels and coordinates before commencement.
Ensure compliance with ES 1717/2008 and project specifications for structural and hydraulic requirements.
Material Inspection:
Concrete: Using approved cement, sand, aggregates, and potable water.
Reinforcement Steel: Confirm diameter, spacing, and bending as per approved bar bending schedule (BBS).
Bricks or Precast Elements (if used): Ensure conformity to ES 1292 or ES 1717 standards for sewer manholes.
Covers and Frames: GRP Covers conforming to ES 2613. Ensure covers are heavy-duty, non-rocking, and properly marked.
Mortar and Waterproofing: Use approved non-shrink grout or sulphate-resistant mortar. Apply waterproofing compounds per specification.
Storage:
Store reinforcement on timber supports off the ground. Cement and admixtures to be kept in dry, covered areas. Covers and frames shall be stored on flat ground away from traffic.
Execution and Installation Methodology
Excavation
Trench Layout and Setting Out
Mark manhole location and pipe centerlines on site according to approved drawings.
Verify levels and alignment with survey instruments.
Excavation
Excavate the manhole pit to required depth and width as per drawings and Egyptian Code for pipes <500 mm.
Side support (shoring) to be provided in all soil types except rock.
Remove unsuitable material and transport to approved disposal areas.
Trench Preparation
Ensure the bottom of the trench is level, clean, and free of debris before placing bedding.
Formwork
Supply and Fabrication
Provide metallic and wooden formwork for foundation, walls, and slabs.
Include openings for pipe inlets/outlets and benching slopes inside the manhole.
Installation
Place formwork on stable support and ensure vertical and horizontal alignment.
Brace formwork adequately to resist concrete pressure.
Verify dimensions according to drawings before pouring concrete.
Reinforced Concrete Works
Foundation and Walls
Pour reinforced concrete for foundation and walls with fair-face finish.
Cement type and mix ratio as per approved soil report and project specifications.
Form benching slope inside manhole to direct flow toward the outlet.
Slab
Install reinforcement steel according to structural drawings.
Pour concrete for slab up to 1.5 m depth.
Ensure proper curing of concrete as per standard procedures.
Waterproofing
Cold-Applied Bitumen
Apply 3 layers of cold-applied bitumen on external walls.
Each layer thickness ≥400 μm after drying.
Allow proper drying between coats.
Ensure continuous coverage without voids.
Manhole Covers and Frames
Covers
Supply and install G.R.P. covers rated for 40-ton wheel load.
Align cover with finished road surface.
Frames (Cast Iron)
Supply gray cast iron frames:
Ø60 cm × 23 cm height for depths ≤3.5 m
Seat frame properly on concrete collar.
Align frame with road level and secure against movement.
Installation Procedure
Clean concrete surface before placing frame.
Place and level the frame.
Install G.R.P. cover and check fit.
Inspect installation before final backfilling.
Ladder Installation
Supply
Cast iron ladder steps weigh 7.25 kg each.
Installation
Fix ladder steps to manhole walls using approved anchors.
Ensure vertical alignment and uniform spacing.
Inspect each step for stability.
Allow only trained personnel to climb ladder.
Backfilling
Material and Layering
Use clean sand for backfill.
Place in layers not exceeding 25 cm loose thickness.
Compaction
Compact each layer following proper engineering methods.
Apply water as required to reach maximum dry density (MDD).
Testing
Conduct field density tests (Sand Cone / Nuclear Gauge) and laboratory compaction tests to verify density.
Continue backfilling to ground or road surface ensuring manhole stability.
Final Checks and Handover
Verify invert levels, alignment, benching slope, slab integrity.
Inspect G.R.P. covers, cast iron frames, and ladder steps.
Ensure compliance with approved drawings and specifications.
Obtain final approval from supervising engineer before commissioning and handover.
Completion and Handover
Upon completion, clean the inside of the manhole, remove debris and formwork remnants.
Inspect dimensions, alignment, and levels against approved drawings.
Prepare as-built records (depth, invert levels, chainage, coordinates).
Obtain consultant/engineer approval prior to permanent backfilling or cover placement.
Testing and Commissioning
Perform visual inspection to ensure all joints, walls, and connections are watertight.
Conduct leak test by filling the manhole with water and observing for visible leakage for a minimum of 30 minutes.
Verify benching smoothness and proper flow through inlet and outlet pipes.
On successful testing, clean the manhole and ensure accessibility of cover.
Safety and Environmental Precautions
All personnel shall wear PPE (helmet, safety boots, reflective vest, gloves, and eye protection).
Install barriers and warning signs around open manholes and excavations.
Ensure proper ventilation when working inside manholes to prevent gas hazards; perform gas detection before entry.
Provide ladders or access steps conforming to ES 1717/2008 requirements.
Dispose of excavated material and concrete waste at approved locations.
Keep the site clean and prevent spillage of concrete or wastewater into surrounding soil or drainage channels.

Method Statement Summary – Storm Water Inlets (Catch Basins)
1. Purpose
The purpose of this work is to ensure the proper construction, installation, and commissioning of storm water inlets embedded in asphalted areas, to provide:
Efficient drainage of rainwater and surface runoff.
Structural stability and durability under traffic loads up to 25 tons.
Prevention of debris and floating materials from entering or exiting the sewer system.
Compliance with approved drawings, project specifications, Egyptian Code, and consultant instructions.
The inlet shall consist of a concrete chamber, finished smooth internally, with an internal barrier to trap floating debris, proper benching slopes to direct water to the outlet, dual cast iron covers with openings, and adequate waterproofing to protect the structure.
2. Execution and Installation Methodology
2.1 Site Preparation and Excavation
Set out the location according to approved drawings.
Excavate to the required depth and dimensions, providing side support in all soil types except rock.
Remove unsuitable material and ensure the excavation base is level and free from debris.
2.2  Concrete Chamber Construction
Form the chamber with plain concrete walls and floor for depths up to 4.5 m; for greater depths, use reinforced concrete per structural drawings.
Construct internal benching slopes to direct flow toward the outlet pipe.
Ensure the floor invert of the outlet pipe is 60 cm above the base.
Achieve a smooth internal surface finish according to specifications.
2.3  Internal Debris Barrier Installation
Install an internal or shared barrier inside the chamber to prevent floating debris from exiting.
Ensure the barrier is securely fixed and does not obstruct water flow.
2.4  Covers and Frames Installation
Supply and install dual cast iron covers with openings, rated for 25-ton wheel load.
Ensure proper seating of frames on the concrete collar.
Align covers flush with the finished asphalt or pavement surface.
Inspect fit and stability before backfilling.
2.5  Waterproofing
Apply two layers of cold-applied oxidized bitumen externally.
Apply approved internal insulation material on the inner walls.
Verify continuous coverage with no gaps or voids.
2.6  Backfilling and Compaction
Backfill with suitable soil or sand in layers ≤25 cm.
Compact each layer using proper techniques to reach maximum dry density (MDD).
Apply water where necessary and perform field and laboratory compaction tests.
Continue backfilling until reaching ground or road level, ensuring chamber stability.
2.7  Final Inspection and Handover
Check invert levels, alignment, benching slopes, covers, frames, and internal barrier.
Verify concrete finish, waterproofing layers, and insulation integrity.
Obtain approval from the supervising engineer prior to commissioning and handover.
