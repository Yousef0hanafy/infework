METHOD STATEMENT FOR WATER NETWORK
METHOD STATEMENT FOR SUPPLY, INSTALLATION, TESTING & COMMISSIONING OF WATER NETWORK INSTALLATION
1. Purpose of constructing water inspection chambers
Ensure efficient operation , monitoring and maintenance of pipeline network . theses chambers provide essential access points that allow operators to inspect the condition of the pipeline , perform routine maintenance , and carry out flushing and disinfection activities whenever required . they also serve as critical locations of installing isolation valves , washout points , and air release valves , enabling effective control and management of water flow within the system.in addition , inspection chambers help in monitoring pressure levels and detecting potential leaks by housing various mechanical fittings and instruments .
.
The scope covers:
Excavation and leveling works to reach the required construction level as per design drawings.
Construction of the reinforced concrete chamber structure, including foundation, walls, and top slab/cover, as per technical specifications.
Supply and installation of valves and special fittings, including:
Air release valves
Isolation valves (Gate valves or Butterfly valves as specified)
Installation of internal pipework, including flanged connections, supports, and all required accessories.
Application of waterproofing systems to the chamber base and walls in accordance with project specifications.
Installation of chamber covers, such as ductile iron or composite covers (GRP) depending on load classification.
Backfilling and compaction works around the chamber after completing installations.
Testing and commissioning, including pressure testing and verification of proper operation of all installed components
2. Preliminary Preparations and Material Inspection
2.1 General Preparations
Review approved IFC drawings, specifications, and project quality plan.
Verify site access, traffic management plans, and work permits.
Identify existing underground utilities using approved detection methods.
Mobilize required manpower, machinery (excavator, compactor, generator, fusion machine), and tools.
2.2 Material Inspection
All materials shall be inspected upon delivery:
concrete
Verify that all materials used in production of concrete comply with project specifications and approved mix design
Check quality and certification of raw materials including cement , aggregates , water and admixtures
Ensure the admixture meet the physical and chemical requirements defined by the standards.
Water proofing
Check that all materials , surfaces and application procedures comply the approved specifications
Ensure that the substrate is properly cleaned , repaired and primed before applying the waterproofing system
Confirm correct application thickness , overlap , bonding and all details around joints and corners
3. Execution and Installation Methodology
3.1 Excavation Works: Excavate to the required depth according to approved drawings and dimensions.
3.2 Sub-base Preparation : Level and compact the base, then place a lean concrete blinding layer if required.
3.3 Formwork & Reinforcement : Install formwork and fix reinforcement for the base slab and walls as per the approved drawings
3.4 concrete casting : Pour concrete for the base slab, followed by the walls and top slab/cover. Ensure proper compaction and curing
3.5 Waterproofing : Apply waterproofing to the external surfaces of the chamber according to specifications
3.6 Internal Works : Install pipes, fittings, benches, channels, and any required accessories inside the chamber.
3.7 Backfilling : After curing and inspection, backfill around the chamber with suitable material and compact in layer.
3.8 Final Inspection : Check the chamber dimensions, levels, waterproofing, and internal works before final approval.
4. Testing and Commissioning
4.1 waterproofing integrity test
Inspect the waterproofing layer and perform a water leakage test (if specified) to ensure no seepage through walls or joints
4.2 Concrete Strength Test
Review concrete compressive strength test results (cubes/cylinders) to confirm compliance with required strength.
4.3 Hydrostatic Test (Water Tightness Test
Fill the chamber with water up to the required level and monitor for leakage over the specified period
4.4 Pipe Connections Test
Check all inlet/outlet pipes, joints, fittings, and seals to ensure proper alignment and no leakage.
4.5 Backfilling Compaction Test
Verify compaction around the chamber through field density tests (e.g., sand cone, nuclear gauge) if required
4.6 Functional Test
Ensure the chamber is accessible, the covers fit properly, and internal channels/benches allow proper flow
