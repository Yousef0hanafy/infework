Method Statement for Sewage Network
Method Statement for Laying of UPVC Sewer Pipes (Ø160 mm, Ø200 mm, Ø250 mm)
Purpose and Scope
This method statement describes the procedure for the installation of underground UPVC pipes with diameters 160 mm, 200 mm, and 250 mm for the sewage network. The work includes handling, laying, jointing, alignment, and bedding of pipes as per the approved drawings, project specifications, and relevant standards (ES 1717/2008).
Preliminary Preparations and Material Inspection
Drawing Review
Review all approved construction drawings and cross-check pipe routes, invert levels, and manhole connections before starting the work. Confirm that the trench alignment and depth conform to design requirements.
Material Inspection
Ensure that all UPVC pipes, fittings, and rubber ring joints are inspected upon delivery for size, wall thickness, and surface defects. Verify compliance with approved material submittals. Damaged or deformed pipes shall be clearly marked and rejected.
Storage
Pipes shall be stored on a firm, flat surface in a shaded area away from direct sunlight and sharp objects. Stacking height shall not exceed the manufacturer’s recommendation. Rubber rings and fittings shall be stored in clean, dry containers to prevent deformation or contamination.
Execution and Installation Methodology
Ensure trench excavation and bedding are completed and approved before starting pipe laying.
Place and level the bedding layer with approved granular material (typically sand) to the required thickness and grade.
Lower UPVC pipes into the trench using ropes, slings, or mechanical lifting devices — never drop or drag pipes.
Align pipes accurately using a laser or spirit level to maintain the designed slope.
Clean and lubricate the spigot and socket ends. Insert the spigot into the socket until the insertion mark reaches the socket edge.
Check that the rubber ring is properly seated to ensure watertight joints.
Install pipes progressively from downstream (low point) to upstream (high point).
Backfill with fine selected material up to 150 mm above the crown of the pipe in layers, compacted manually or mechanically to avoid pipe displacement.
Completion and Handover
Upon completion of laying and initial backfilling, record as-built data including alignment, invert levels, and manhole connection points.
The area shall be cleaned and cleared of waste material. Final inspection shall be conducted jointly with the consultant or supervising engineer. Any non-conformities shall be rectified prior to handover.
Testing and Commissioning
Perform leakage tests (exfiltration or infiltration) in accordance with ES 1717/2008 or project specifications.
Testing shall be witnessed and approved by the consultant.
Ensure all connections are watertight and free from deformation or blockage.
After successful testing, flush the pipeline with clean water to remove debris and prepare for final commissioning.
Safety and Environmental Precautions
All personnel shall wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) such as safety helmets, reflective vests, gloves, safety boots, and eye protection.
Excavations deeper than 1.5 m shall be adequately shored or sloped to prevent collapse.
Barricades, warning signs, and lighting shall be provided around open trenches to protect workers and the public.
Heavy equipment shall be operated only by certified operators, maintaining a safe distance from trench edges.
Dispose of excavated material, packaging, and debris at approved disposal sites in accordance with environmental regulations.
Ensure proper drainage to prevent water accumulation in open trenches, reducing risk of instability and contamination.
Method Statement for Construction of Concrete Manholes (Ø1.0 m & Ø1.2 m) According to Egyptian Standard ES 1717/2008
Purpose and Scope
This method statement outlines the procedure for construction of circular reinforced concrete manholes with internal diameters of 1.0 m (depth ≤ 1.5 m) and 1.2 m (depth 1.51 – 2.5 m) for the sewage network, in accordance with the Egyptian Standard Specification ES 1717/2008 and approved project drawings.
The scope includes excavation, base preparation, formwork, reinforcement, concrete works, benching, pipe connection, finishing, cover installation, inspection, and testing.
Preliminary Preparations and Material Inspection
Drawing and Specification Review
Review approved construction drawings for manhole location, size, depth, and connection details with incoming and outgoing pipes. Confirm invert levels and coordinates before commencement.
Ensure compliance with ES 1717/2008 and project specifications for structural and hydraulic requirements.
Material Inspection:
Concrete: Using approved cement, sand, aggregates, and potable water.
Reinforcement Steel: Confirm diameter, spacing, and bending as per approved bar bending schedule (BBS).
Bricks or Precast Elements (if used): Ensure conformity to ES 1292 or ES 1717 standards for sewer manholes.
Covers and Frames: GRP Covers conforming to ES 2613. Ensure covers are heavy-duty, non-rocking, and properly marked.
Mortar and Waterproofing: Use approved non-shrink grout or sulphate-resistant mortar. Apply waterproofing compounds per specification.
Storage:
Store reinforcement on timber supports off the ground. Cement and admixtures to be kept in dry, covered areas. Covers and frames shall be stored on flat ground away from traffic.
Execution and Installation Methodology
a. Excavation and Base Preparation:
Excavate to the required dimensions and depth as per the approved drawings.
The bottom of excavation shall be level and firm. Where unstable soil is encountered, replace with compacted granular material.
Provide a blinding layer (plain concrete, 50–75 mm thick) before placing reinforcement for the base slab.
b. Base Slab Construction:
Place reinforcement as per approved drawings.
Pour reinforced concrete base slab (minimum thickness = 150 mm for Ø1.0 m and 200 mm for Ø1.2 m manholes).
Level and finish the surface smooth; ensure correct invert level of incoming/outgoing pipes.
c. Manhole Wall Construction:
Construct circular reinforced concrete wall (minimum wall thickness 150–200 mm depending on manhole type).
Place formwork securely to ensure accurate dimensions and verticality.
Pour concrete continuously to avoid cold joints. Use vibrators for proper compaction.
For precast sections, install elements with proper joint sealing using approved rubber gaskets or mortar joints (in compliance with ES 1717).
d. Benching and Channels:
After curing of the wall, construct the benching with smooth finished mortar ensuring self-cleansing gradient towards the outlet.
Channel shape and size shall match pipe diameter.
e. Cover Slab and Frame Installation:
Cast or place the reinforced concrete cover slab with appropriate openings for the manhole cover.
Set the frame in position with mortar, ensuring correct alignment with ground level and surrounding surface.
f. Pipe Connections:
Connect incoming and outgoing pipes to the manhole wall with watertight joints.
Seal around the pipe using non-shrink grout and approved waterproofing sealant.
g. Curing and Finishing:
Cure concrete for a minimum of 7 days (or per specification).
Apply internal waterproofing (bituminous or cementitious coating) as required.
h. Backfilling:
After approval, backfill around the manhole with selected material in layers not exceeding 300 mm, compacted to 95% MDD.
Completion and Handover
Upon completion, clean the inside of the manhole, remove debris and formwork remnants.
Inspect dimensions, alignment, and levels against approved drawings.
Prepare as-built records (depth, invert levels, chainage, coordinates).
Obtain consultant/engineer approval prior to permanent backfilling or cover placement.
Testing and Commissioning
Perform visual inspection to ensure all joints, walls, and connections are watertight.
Conduct leak test by filling the manhole with water and observing for visible leakage for a minimum of 30 minutes.
Verify benching smoothness and proper flow through inlet and outlet pipes.
On successful testing, clean the manhole and ensure accessibility of cover.
Safety and Environmental Precautions
All personnel shall wear PPE (helmet, safety boots, reflective vest, gloves, and eye protection).
Install barriers and warning signs around open manholes and excavations.
Ensure proper ventilation when working inside manholes to prevent gas hazards; perform gas detection before entry.
Provide ladders or access steps conforming to ES 1717/2008 requirements.
Dispose of excavated material and concrete waste at approved locations.
Keep the site clean and prevent spillage of concrete or wastewater into surrounding soil or drainage channels.
Method Statement for Construction of Inspection Chambers According to Egyptian Standard ES 1717/2008
Purpose and Scope
This method statement defines the procedure for the construction of inspection chambers (for house connections and shallow sewer lines) in accordance with the Egyptian Standard Specification ES 1717/2008 and the approved project drawings.
The scope covers excavation, base preparation, construction of chamber walls and benching, installation of covers, pipe connections, inspection, and final handover.
Preliminary Preparations and Material Inspection
Drawing and Specification Review:
Review approved drawings to confirm the location, depth, internal dimensions, and invert levels of the inspection chambers.
Ensure that the details comply with ES 1717/2008 and project design criteria, including connection angles, cover type, and invert fall.
Material Inspection:
Concrete: Minimum grade C25/30 for base and walls.
Masonry Units (if used): Solid bricks or concrete blocks in compliance with ES 1292 and ES 1717.
Reinforcement Steel: Confirm size, spacing, and quantity as per approved structural drawings.
Covers and Frames: GRP conforming to ES 2613; non-rocking, watertight, and corrosion resistant.
Mortar: Sulphate-resistant cement mortar (1:3) mix for joints and plastering.
Waterproofing: Bituminous or cementitious coating applied internally after curing.
Storage:
Store bricks, pipes, and fittings on level ground, away from water and contamination.
Keep cement and admixtures in a dry, covered area.
Covers and frames should be handled carefully to prevent edge damage.
Execution and Installation Methodology
a. Excavation and Base Preparation:
Excavate to the required size and depth as per approved drawings.
The bottom of excavation must be firm and level; if soft soil is encountered, replace it with compacted sand or granular material.
Provide a plain concrete blinding layer (50–75 mm thick) before the reinforced base slab is cast.
b. Base Slab Construction:
Place reinforcement as per approved details.
Pour reinforced concrete base slab (minimum thickness 100–150 mm depending on depth).
Ensure the surface is level and at correct elevation for pipe invert levels.
c. Wall Construction:
Construct walls in solid brick masonry or reinforced concrete as specified.
Wall thickness not less than 120 mm for brick or 150 mm for concrete.
Apply internal plastering (12 mm thick) in cement-sand mortar with smooth finish.
d. Benching and Channel Formation:
After wall construction and curing, form the internal channel to match the diameter of incoming and outgoing pipes.
Benching shall have a smooth mortar finish and self-cleansing slope towards the outlet pipe.
e. Cover Slab and Frame Installation:
Cast reinforced concrete cover slab with opening suitable for the inspection cover.
Install the frame and cover properly aligned with finished ground level.
Seal around the frame with mortar to prevent infiltration.
f. Pipe Connection:
Connect pipes through wall openings using watertight joints.
Fill gaps around pipe entry with non-shrink grout and waterproof coating.
g. Curing and Finishing:
Cure concrete and mortar work for a minimum of 7 days.
Apply waterproof coating internally after curing is completed.
h. Backfilling:
Backfill around the chamber in layers not exceeding 300 mm using selected material compacted to 95% MDD.
Avoid impact or vibration on chamber walls during compaction.
Completion and Handover
Clean and inspect the chamber internally, ensuring smooth benching and proper alignment of inlets/outlets.
Remove debris, shuttering, and excess mortar.
Record chamber dimensions, depth, invert levels, and coordinates for as-built drawings.
Obtain inspection approval from the consultant prior to final acceptance and cover installation.
Testing and Commissioning
Perform water leakage test by filling the chamber to the cover level and observing for leakage for at least 30 minutes.
Verify that flow through the chamber is unobstructed and aligned with connected pipes.
After successful testing, flush connected pipes with clean water.
Safety and Environmental Precautions
All personnel shall wear PPE (safety helmets, gloves, reflective vests, boots, and eye protection).
Provide barricades and warning signage around open excavations.
When depth exceeds 1.5 m, use shoring or sloping to prevent collapse.
Ensure proper ventilation before entry and check for toxic gases if confined space entry is required.
Keep the site free from construction waste, oil, and cement residues.
Dispose of excess material in approved locations as per environmental regulations.
