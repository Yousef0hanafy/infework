Purpose	:	To provide a complete motor control centre, pre-wired and ready for Installation.
Quantity		:	One
Type	:	Free standing control panel completely assembled, wired and tested at the factory. The panel shall be fabricated in sheet steel, shaped and reinforced to form a rigid front access enclosure. The panel shall be for indoor use.
Mounting 	:	The panel should be complete with all pushbuttons, fuses, selector switches, contractors, relays, indicating lights, and timers etc. to form a local central control station.
Cabling	:	Wiring shall be brought out to screw type terminal block, labelled and identified in accordance with wiring diagram.
Nameplates 	:	All equipment and major devices shall be provided with nameplates in English engraved plastic and all pushbuttons and selector switches shall be appropriately tagged.
Finish	:	All steel work shall be bowdlerised or given similar treatment before priming and painting with epoxy paint to a smooth finish.
Lights	:	Alarms, indicator lights and other auxiliaries.
Starters	:	The panel shall accommodate starting contractors for all motors.
Main Power supply	:	380 Volt / 3 phase / 50 cycles.
