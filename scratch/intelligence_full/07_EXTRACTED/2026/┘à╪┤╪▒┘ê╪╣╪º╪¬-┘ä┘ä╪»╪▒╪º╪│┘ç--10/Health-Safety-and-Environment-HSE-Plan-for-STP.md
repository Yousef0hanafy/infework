Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) Plan
For 500  STP Upgrade
Gas Monitoring & Confined Space Entry
Since this is an existing STP, the presence of Hydrogen Sulfide () and Methane () is a primary threat.
Gas Detection: A calibrated 4-Gas Monitor must be used before and during any entry into the Equalization or Aeration tanks.
Action Level: If  exceeds 10 ppm, the area must be evacuated immediately.
Continuous Ventilation: Mechanical blowers must provide at least 20 air changes per hour during the installation of the diffuser grid.
Standby Person: A dedicated safety watchman must remain at the tank opening, equipped with a tripod and a mechanical winch for emergency extraction.
Biological Hazard Management
Working with existing sewage exposes workers to pathogens (E. coli, Parasites, Viruses).
Mandatory PPE: Waterproof chemical-resistant coveralls, nitrile inner gloves with heavy-duty outer gloves, and N95 masks or full-face respirators.
Decontamination Zone: A designated area for "Donning and Doffing" (putting on/taking off) PPE. Workers must wash with antimicrobial soap before leaving the site.
Immunization: All site personnel must provide proof of vaccination for Hepatitis A, B and Tetanus.
LOTO (Lock-Out / Tag-Out) & Tie-in Safety
Integrating the new PLC Panel with the existing electrical system requires strict isolation.
Electrical Isolation: The existing main breaker must be locked with a unique padlock and a Danger Tag identifying the authorized engineer.
Verification: A "Test-Before-Touch" policy will be enforced using a calibrated voltmeter to ensure zero energy before starting any wiring.
Mechanical & Pressurized System Safety
Blower Startup: During the commissioning of the 350 Rotary Blowers, a safety zone must be established to guard against noise (>85dB) and potential pipe bursts.
Sludge Pump Protection: The Progressive Cavity Pump must have a pressure relief valve and a dry-run sensor installed and tested before the first "Wet Run" to prevent stator overheating or pipe rupture.
Waste & Sludge Handling
Containment: Any sludge removed during the upgrade of the aeration system must be placed in sealed containers or skip-bins to prevent odor complaints and soil contamination.
Housekeeping: Daily site cleaning is mandatory to prevent the accumulation of sewage-soaked debris, which attracts pests and increases biological risks.
