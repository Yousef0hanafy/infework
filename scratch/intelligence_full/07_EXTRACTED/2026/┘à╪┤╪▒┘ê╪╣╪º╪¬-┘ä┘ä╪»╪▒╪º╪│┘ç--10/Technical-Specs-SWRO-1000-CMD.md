Desalination Plant SWRO 1000 m3/day
TABLE OF CONTENTS
Design, Supply, installation commissioning and guarantee for the capacity and product purity at any time of the year according to the following conditions:
Maximum allowable pressure shall be about 70 bar. Pressure shall be reduced at the higher temperature range if the membrane characteristics would so require, the membranes used in the design shall be of the spiral wound type
Design Parameters
Feed water type	: Wells
Membrane Design period	: 3 years with membrane replacement
Feed temperature	: 18 ÷ 32 °C
Silt density index in RO-feed (SDI_15)	: < 3
Total RO recovery	: 35-40 %
Media filters	Filtering rate	: ≤ 10 m/h at all filter in service
Backwash rate	: 25 m3/h
Cartridge filters (Specific flow rate)	: ≤ 2.6 m3/h/40˝ cartridge
Average flux	: 16 (l/m2 /hr.)
High pressure pipes velocity	: 3 : 4 m/sec
Low pressure pipes velocity	: 1.5 : 2.5 m/sec
Design Basis
The sea water reverse osmosis plant is designed to operate on sea water Beach wells with a feed TDS of 40,000 mg/l maximum with a water analysis as described
The unit daily net capacity is capable of producing the needed capacity for each site from potable water over a 24 hours operating period per day after one year of operation, allowing preparation of chemical solutions and general maintenance purposes.
The reverse osmosis unit shall be designed to produce the stated capacity and product quality after one year operation when treating water with a TDS of 40000 mg/l at a design temperature of 28 °C.
