Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE) Plan
For 1000 /D SWRO Plant
This section outlines the safety protocols to be implemented during the installation, testing, and operation phases to ensure a zero-accident environment.
High-Pressure Safety
The RO system operates under extreme pressure (up to 45-60 bar).
Pressure Testing: All high-pressure lines (SS316L) must undergo hydrostatic testing at 1.5x the operating pressure. The area must be cordoned off with safety tapes, and only essential personnel allowed during the test.
Safety Relief Valves (SRV): Mechanical SRVs must be installed on the discharge of the High-Pressure Pump and the ERD to prevent vessel over-pressurization.
Flexible Couplings: All Victaulic couplings on the 11 FRP Vessels must be checked for correct seating to prevent "missile-like" blowouts under pressure.
Chemical Handling & Hazardous Materials
The plant uses Antiscalant, Sodium Metabisulfite (SMBS), and Chlorine/Acid.
Spill Containment: All Injection Dosing Tanks must be placed within a Bund Wall or secondary containment pallet to catch any leaks.
Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Staff must wear chemical goggles, face shields, and acid-resistant gloves when handling or mixing chemicals.
Safety Showers: An emergency eyewash/shower station must be installed near the dosing and CIP (Flushing) area.
MSDS: Material Safety Data Sheets for all chemicals must be displayed in English and the local language.
Electrical & Automation Safety
LOTO (Lock-Out/Tag-Out): Strict LOTO procedures must be applied to the Control Panel and VFD during any maintenance or wiring to prevent accidental motor startup.
Earthing (Grounding): The RO Support Skid and all metallic tanks must be properly grounded to prevent static electricity discharge, especially in a saline (conductive) environment.
IP Ratings: All junction boxes and instruments must maintain their IP55/65 rating to prevent water ingress and short circuits.
Mechanical & Lifting Safety
Lifting Plan: A formal lifting plan is required for the installation of the RO Skid and Multimedia Filters. Use of certified slings and shackles only.
Rotating Equipment: The Filter Feed Pump and High-Pressure Pump must have coupling guards always installed to prevent entanglement.
Emergency Shutdown (ESD) System
Emergency Stop Buttons: Strategically placed E-Stop buttons on the Control Panel and near the High-Pressure Pump.
PLC Fail-Safe: The PLC logic is programmed to trigger an immediate shutdown if:
Feed pressure drops below the safety limit (to prevent pump cavitation).
Permeate pressure exceeds 2 bar (to prevent membrane rupture).
The VFD detects an electrical fault.
Environmental Protection
Brine Disposal: Ensure the concentrated brine discharge follows the environmental permit to avoid damage to the local ecosystem.
Noise Control: The High-Pressure Pump and VFD are housed to ensure noise levels stay within the industrial limit (typically <85 dB).
