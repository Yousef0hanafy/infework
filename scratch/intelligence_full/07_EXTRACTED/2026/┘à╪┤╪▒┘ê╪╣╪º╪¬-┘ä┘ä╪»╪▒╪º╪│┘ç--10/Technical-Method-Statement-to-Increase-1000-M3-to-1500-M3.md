# Technical Method Statement to Increase 1000 M3 to 1500 M3.docx

> هذا المحتوى مستخرج آليًا من الملف الأصلي. يجب الرجوع إلى المصدر عند الاعتماد على رقم أو قيمة.

Technical Method Statement to Increase 1000  To 1500  SWRO
Upgrading the plant capacity from 1000  To 1500  while maintaining continuous operation (Zero Downtime).
Hydraulic & Design Validation
Hydraulic Modeling: Run new membrane projections to verify that the existing high-pressure pump (HPP) and energy recovery device (ERD) can handle the 50% increase in feed and brine flow.
VFD Adjustment: Recalculate the VFD parameters for the HPP to provide the extra head and flow required for the additional 500
Scope of Expansion (Supply & Installation)
Pressure Vessels: Addition of approximately 5 to 6 new FRP Vessels (8-inch, 6-element each) to reach the 1500   target.
Membrane Loading: Installation of 30-36 new high-rejection Sea Water membranes.
Interconnecting Piping: Using high-pressure SS316L manifolds for the extension to be linked to the existing skid.
Installation Methodology (Step-by-Step)
Off-Site Fabrication: The expansion frame and piping manifolds will be fabricated off-site to minimize on-site hot work and downtime.
Structural Integration: Securing the new vessel racks to the existing RO Support Skid.
Tie-in Procedure: Pre-install Isolation Valves at the suction and discharge headers.
Execute the final connection during low-demand hours. The existing 1000  stream remains pressurized while the new 500  branch is integrated.
Electrical & Control Update: Modify the PLC Logic to monitor the increased flow rates and adjust dosing chemical rates (Antiscalant/SMBS) proportionally.
Update the HMI to display the new total production of 1500 .
Commissioning of the Expanded System
Line Flushing: Flush the new section with low-pressure water to remove debris.
Stabilization: Gradually increase the VFD frequency to bring the total plant output to 1500 .
Performance Monitoring: Verify that salt rejection remains at 98-99% and that the differential pressure () across the new and old membranes is within design limits.
Technical Schedule for Transition: 1000  to 1500
Technical Method Statement for Integration
Hydraulic Balancing: Before connecting the new vessels, Perform a Hydraulic Calculation to ensure that the flow distribution remains equal between the old and new vessels.
Manifold Extension: Use Orbital Welding or high-quality TIG welding for the SS316L high-pressure manifold extension to ensure maximum durability under the new 45-50 bar pressure.
Zero-Downtime Tie-in: The new extension will be fully assembled and pressure-tested independently.
