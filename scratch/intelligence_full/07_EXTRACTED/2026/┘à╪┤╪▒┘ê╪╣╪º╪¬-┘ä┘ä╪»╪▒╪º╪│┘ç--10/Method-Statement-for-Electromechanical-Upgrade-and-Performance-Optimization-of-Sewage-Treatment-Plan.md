Method Statement for Electromechanical Upgrade and Performance Optimization of Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) - 500
Process Design and Engineering
Assessment: Conduct a site survey of the existing civil structures to ensure the new screens and blowers fit the current dimensions.
Engineering: Preparation of updated P&ID and Electrical SLD (Single Line Diagram) to integrate the new PLC panel with the existing control system.
Automation Logic: Designing the control sequence for the Equalization Tank pumps and the DO-linked aeration control.
Electromechanical Works
Screening System (Stainless Steel)
Manual Screen: Installation of a coarse SS manual screen (Opening >= 50mm). It will be anchored in the bypass channel to allow flow diversion during mechanical screen maintenance.
Mechanical Screen: Installation of an automatic fine SS screen (Opening >= 10 mm).
Pre-installation: Drain the channel or divert the flow to the bypass. Check the concrete walls for verticality and smoothness.
Positioning: Use a crane or manual hoist to lower the Mechanical Screen. Ensure the frame sits perfectly on the channel "bench."
Fixing: Drill and install SS316 expansion/chemical anchors. Apply non-shrink grout (like SikaGrout) between the screen frame and the concrete to prevent "Short-circuiting" (water passing around the screen).
Automation: Connected to the control panel to operate based on time or differential water level.
Equalization Tank Submersible Pumps
Specifications: Supply of (1 Duty / 1 Standby) SS Submersible Pumps (24 @ 12m head).
Guide Rail Setup: Secure the Base Discharge Elbow to the tank floor. Fix the upper guide rail bracket to the tank walkway.
Sealing: Lower the pump using a SS316 chain; the pump's weight and the "Auto-coupling" flange must create a water-tight seal with the base elbow.
Level Control: Three-level float switches (Low: Cut-out, High: Start, High-High: Alarm/Duty-Assist).
Aeration & Mixing System
Diffuser Network: Enhancing the air distribution grid with fine bubble diffusers for high oxygen transfer.
Air Mixers: Installation of mixers to ensure a homogeneous mixture and prevent sludge settling in the corners, effectively eliminating anaerobic pockets and odors.
Rotary Air Blowers
Specs: 2 Blowers (350  @ 0.5 bar pressure).
Blower Placement: Install blowers on a leveled concrete plinth with Anti-vibration pads. Connect the discharge using a Flexible Connector to prevent vibration transfer to the piping.
Piping: Connection to the new aeration grid with check valves and pressure relief valves.
Sludge Transfer Pump (Progressive Cavity)
Type: Positive Displacement (12  @ 4 bar head).
Foundation: Anchor the pump skid to the floor.
Alignment: Use a Dial Indicator to check the alignment between the motor and the pump shaft. Misalignment in PC pumps leads to rapid stator wear.
Application: Used for thickened sludge transfer.
Startup: Ensure the pump is primed with liquid before running to prevent stator damage (Dry running protection).
Chemical Injection Systems
Package: Integrated tanks and dosing pumps for odor control (e.g., Ferric Chloride or Oxidizing agents).
Piping: Chemical-resistant tubing (UPVC) secured to cable trays.
Instrumentation & Control System
Field Instruments
Flow Meters: Installation of electromagnetic flow meters for raw sewage and treated effluent.
DO Sensors: Dissolved Oxygen probes installed in the aeration tank to provide feedback to the blowers/VFDs.
pH & Turbidity: Online sensors at the final effluent point to ensure compliance.
New Control Panel (PLC/HMI)
Integration: The new panel will be linked to the existing panel via a communication bridge
HMI: A touchscreen interface will display real-time trends of Oxygen levels, Pump status, and Alarms.
Commissioning & Startup
Mechanical & Electrical Checks (Dry Run)
I/O Testing: Force each signal from the PLC to ensure the screens rotate and the blowers start.
Rotation Check: "Bump" the motors to confirm the correct direction of rotation (especially for Blowers and PC pumps).
Instrument Calibration: Calibrate the DO Sensor in open air and the Flow Meter according to the manufacturer's zero-point setting.
Functional Testing (Wet Run)
Screen Automation: Simulate a high level in the channel to verify the mechanical screen starts automatically.
Aeration Balancing: Start the Rotary Blowers. Adjust the manual valves on the air laterals until the bubble pattern is uniform in the aeration tank.
Dosing System: Fill the tanks with clean water first. Run the Dosing Pumps to check for leaks in the tubing and verify the injection rate (L/hr).
Process Startup (Stabilization)
DO Loop: Verify that when the Dissolved Oxygen sensor reaches 2.0 mg/L, the VFD reduces the blower speed (Energy saving mode).
Sludge Transfer: Run the Progressive Cavity Pump. Monitor the discharge pressure and motor current to ensure the sludge is moving without clogging.
Odor Control: Monitor the H2S levels around the tank; the combination of the new air mixers and chemical dosing should show a reduction in smell within 24-48 hours.
